About Us

On July 8, 2015, our minority family's lives were drastically and permanently changed by nine racist words. "Your family has no rights; planes have no rules." They were spoken proudly by the airport manager, Meadow Chase, who was responsible for the management of Ramona Airport.

Meadow Chase was already fully aware of the impact her words would have and had the backing of local public servants and co-conspirators. Unwittingly, we found ourselves in a vicious, hopeless, helpless landscape of fear and violence, unable to use our property for its intended purpose or live in peace, as we had done for the past fifteen years.

Author's Note

Some of the bad actors featured in this books’ names do not appear, and their identifying information is blurred in the pictures. We made the decision to focus on public servants who preyed on us and other vulnerable members of our community. This was not a decision taken lightly. Although private citizens did contribute to our suffering, either in collaboration with public employees or separately, this book is intended to point out the institutions and the people who ran them for their bigotry. Individuals may act badly of their own volition, but local, state, and federal agencies should be held to a higher standard.

It is the violation of those standards and the terrible consequences of those numerous and repeated violations that are the core of our story.
